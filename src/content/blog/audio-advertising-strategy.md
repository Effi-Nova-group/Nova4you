Audio has quietly become one of the most crowded rooms in media. Commercial radio still reaches the vast majority of Australians every week, podcast listening keeps climbing, and music streaming services follow people from the kitchen to the car to the gym. Yet many brands still plan these as three separate line items with three separate briefs. That's a missed opportunity.

## Why Audio Works

Audio reaches people in moments when screens can't: driving, cooking, working out, commuting. It's also an intimate medium. A trusted presenter or podcast host speaking directly into someone's ears builds a kind of credibility that a banner ad never will. Listeners don't skip through it the way they scroll past visual content, and a strong sonic identity sticks in memory long after the ad ends.

## Three Formats, One Listener

### Broadcast Radio

Radio delivers scale and frequency at a cost few channels can match. It's ideal for local reach, time-sensitive offers and building familiarity through repetition. Live reads from on-air talent add borrowed trust that recorded spots can't.

### Podcasts

Podcasts offer depth and niche targeting. Audiences choose shows that match their interests, so a brand can reach small business owners, health-conscious parents or motorsport fans with precision. Host-read ads in particular tend to drive strong recall and response.

### Streaming Audio

Music and audio streaming platforms bring digital targeting to the medium: age, location, device, time of day and listening mood. Programmatic buying lets brands adjust messaging in real time and pair audio with companion display ads.

## Planning Audio as One Channel

A connected audio strategy shares a single creative idea across formats while respecting how each one is consumed:

- **Build a sonic identity**: a consistent voice, sound or jingle makes every placement reinforce the last.
- **Match format to message**: use radio for reach and urgency, podcasts for storytelling, streaming for targeted follow-up.
- **Sequence the story**: broad radio awareness can be followed by targeted streaming messages to the same regions or audiences.
- **Measure response**: unique offer codes, vanity URLs, search lift and brand-lift studies show what each format contributes.

## The Takeaway

Audio is no longer a single channel. It's an ecosystem that follows your customer through their whole day. Brands that plan radio, podcasts and streaming together, with a consistent sound and a shared measurement framework, turn listening time into lasting trust and measurable growth.
